About Manobandhu Foundation

A beacon of hope for the mentally ill destitute, working tirelessly since October 2021

Our Journey

'Manobandhu' began as a campaign to rehabilitate wandering mentally ill destitute persons, starting operations on 26th October 2021. Our mission is to connect these vulnerable individuals with existing mental care facilities for proper rehabilitation.

Registered as a trust on 27th June 2022, we work closely with state functionaries across both Telugu states to ensure every person receives the care and support they desperately need.

Our Purpose

Driven by compassion and guided by purpose, we work tirelessly to transform lives and restore hope.

Our Vision

To make Telugu states a role model in rescue and rehabilitation of destitute persons, transforming lives through collaborative efforts.

Our Mission

Connect wandering mentally ill individuals with mental care facilities, working with state functionaries to ensure proper care and support.

Our Values

Compassion, dignity, and human rights guide every action we take in our mission to restore hope and reunite families.

Our Impact

Empowering individuals to lead dignified lives while securing their fundamental human rights through comprehensive rehabilitation.
